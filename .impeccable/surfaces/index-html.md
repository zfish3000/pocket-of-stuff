# Home surface

Mode: Experience. Audience: visitors exploring Zachary Fisher's work. Job: scan project names, play with the construction if desired, and open any project reliably. Primary action: choose a project block. Constraints: semantic links, one-file project discovery, reduced motion, no invented work, and no copied Monument Valley composition.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is the construction itself, not a conventional hero followed by a card grid. A deliberate tower makes project discovery feel like handling a small personal object.

OWN-WORLD: A warm assembly mat holds flat-color 2D blocks in coral, aqua, lilac, plum, amber, and powder. Per the user's latest direction, blocks have no wood texture or drop shadow. Heavy condensed Archivo labels stay attached to the pieces; restrained graphite marks and Plex Mono utilities stay at the edges.

STORY: The visitor immediately sees Zachary's projects assembled into something playable, understands that each labeled piece opens work, and may pull one loose to let the construction respond physically.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact upper-left wordmark stays subordinate to a large, visibly supported central construction. The physics field fills the viewport; the instruction sits lower-left and Rebuild appears lower-right only after displacement.

VIEWPORT: The page cannot scroll. The construction scales to fit the viewport and rigid blocks collide with visible screen boundaries. Native link dragging is disabled; click navigation remains available.

FORM: “The Toymaker's Assembly Mat,” selected directly with the user before a concept-seed record was created. Historical list position and seed key are unrecorded; this absence is preserved rather than reconstructed.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
